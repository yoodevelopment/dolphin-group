# Product Marketing Page

## Identify the page job

Choose one dominant job:
- explain a capability;
- persuade a segment;
- compare plans;
- demonstrate an integration;
- resolve objections;
- support evaluation.

Do not make every subpage a smaller copy of the homepage.

## Structure by evaluation need

Feature page:
- problem context;
- mechanism;
- product evidence;
- workflow;
- outcome;
- limitations or fit;
- action.

Solution page:
- segment-specific pains;
- role workflow;
- proof for that segment;
- implementation;
- action.

Integration page:
- systems connected;
- setup;
- data flow;
- examples;
- permissions/security;
- action.

Pricing page:
- decision model;
- plan differences;
- total-cost clarity;
- limits;
- FAQ;
- purchase/contact action.

## Visual strategy

Use the product as evidence:
- real UI;
- diagrams;
- annotated flows;
- scenario-specific snapshots;
- data states.

Avoid decorative mockups unrelated to the feature being explained.
