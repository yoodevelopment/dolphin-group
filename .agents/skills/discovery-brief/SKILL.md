# Discovery and Brief

## Goal

Create enough shared clarity to design without relying on generic SaaS assumptions.

## Extract from available context

- product and category;
- audience and level of awareness;
- page type;
- primary action;
- business model;
- key proof;
- required content;
- technical stack;
- brand cues;
- legal or factual constraints.

## Resolve missing information

When interaction is possible, ask only high-leverage concrete questions. Otherwise use placeholders and label assumptions.

Prioritize:
1. What action should the visitor take?
2. What exact value do they receive?
3. Why should they believe it?
4. What content and assets already exist?
5. What must not be invented?

## Content inventory

Classify each item:
- confirmed;
- needs editing;
- missing but required;
- optional;
- prohibited to fabricate.

Do not use fake testimonials, fabricated logos, or invented numbers to make the layout look complete.

## Design framing output

Produce:
- one-sentence page goal;
- audience;
- primary conversion;
- content hierarchy;
- evidence hierarchy;
- visual adjectives;
- technical constraints;
- assumptions.

Write this into `PROJECT-BRIEF.md` when asked or use it directly for the current task.
