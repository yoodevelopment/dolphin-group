# Asset Direction

## Inventory first

Find:
- logos;
- screenshots;
- illustrations;
- photography;
- icons;
- diagrams;
- brand textures;
- existing animation;
- legal usage constraints.

## Choose evidence over decoration

Prioritize:
1. real product interface;
2. real customer or operational artifact;
3. explanatory diagram;
4. data visualization;
5. art-directed photography;
6. abstract decoration.

## Screenshot treatment

- crop around the claim;
- redact sensitive information;
- maintain readable scale;
- use consistent framing;
- avoid tiny full-app screenshots;
- annotate only when annotation adds understanding.

## Diagrams

Use simple visual grammar:
- nodes;
- links;
- sequence;
- boundaries;
- state;
- transformation.

Provide a text equivalent for important meaning.

## SVG motifs

Create a small reusable system:
- line weight;
- corner treatment;
- grid;
- motion;
- color roles.

Do not download or imitate copyrighted artwork without permission.

## Placeholders

When assets are missing, use clearly labeled neutral placeholders or product-specific CSS/SVG concepts. Do not fabricate customer evidence.
