# Conversion Content Structure

## Build around questions

At each stage answer the visitor's likely question:
- relevance: Is this for me?
- value: What changes?
- mechanism: How?
- credibility: Why believe it?
- fit: Is it compatible with my situation?
- risk: What could go wrong?
- action: What happens next?

## Headline quality

Prefer:
- concrete nouns;
- active verbs;
- customer outcome;
- meaningful distinction;
- specific scope.

Avoid inflated language, category clichés, and unsupported superlatives.

## Evidence placement

Place proof next to the claim it supports. A logo wall at the top cannot substantiate every claim below it.

## CTA labels

Name the next step:
- Start free;
- View live demo;
- Compare plans;
- Book a 20-minute call;
- Download the guide.

Avoid vague labels such as “Learn more” when a more specific label exists.

## Honesty

Do not:
- invent urgency;
- hide price qualifiers;
- fabricate social proof;
- imply security certification not provided;
- present estimates as guarantees.

## Visual content design

Make content scannable through:
- short section thesis;
- evidence;
- supporting detail;
- action.

Do not solve weak copy by shrinking it into decorative labels.
